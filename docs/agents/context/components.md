# Component Context

- **Pause presentation** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CameraRig.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`): Camera stops and hides the interaction tooltip during Escape pause; tooltip background follows the shared transparency setting.

- **Atlas pause and camp placement** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CarModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Crates.tsx`): Escape opens the pause menu and pauses movement, interaction and crates, clears held input. Closing/unmounting resumes. The atlas stays live. Camp is offset from the player spawn to avoid overlapping its monument.

- **Camp rescue reset** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/CarModel.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`): Explicit mutable teleport revision resets velocity and rollover even when R returns the car from within five units of camp.

- **Portfolio integration** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`): Passes mutable town state to the expedition HUD; renders the car directly without character selection or switching handlers.

- **ExpeditionWorld** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/ExpeditionWorld.tsx`): Camp, five restoration monuments, energy crystals and light columns. Frame-based proximity discovery and height-aware pickups dispatch discrete Effector events; F restores/completes, R rescues to camp. Composed in `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/TownScene.tsx`.

## Page (`src/pages`)

- **PortfolioPage** (`src/pages/Portfolio`): Route `/` — a bright "diorama" top-down town (react-three-fiber). A voxel car moves freely in a room with multiple altar signs; approaching an altar highlights it (pulsing accent ring + light), shows a tooltip, pressing E triggers a transition (the camera slowly dollies to the altar name while the screen fades to white), then navigates to the altar's target route.
  - `lib/TownScene.tsx`: Composes the 3D scene — sky/fog atmosphere, hemisphere + warm sun + cool rim lights, drei `SoftShadows`, `Terrain`, `Props`, `Pedestals`, the 3×3 crate stack, the car, follow camera. Renders `Pedestals` with the mutable town state, and the single `CarModel` with a fixed car collision radius. There is **no fence** — the open world is bounded by the generated 8×6 chunk area.
  - `lib/constants.ts`: World grid tokens (`CHUNK_SIZE` 16, `WORLD_COLS` 6, `WORLD_ROWS` 4, `TERRAIN_SEED`), player spawn position, interaction timing, camera tuning (base/near-pedestal FOV, blend speeds, interaction zoom, `CAMERA_FAR`), and atmosphere tokens (bright cartoon sky `#bfe3f5`, fog near/far, sun position). There is no map-boundary clamp.
  - `lib/Terrain.tsx`: Open-world ground — one height-mapped `PlaneGeometry` mesh per active chunk (always an 8×6 = 48-chunk window). Vertices are displaced by `world.terrain.heightAt`; per-vertex colors blend the biome `low`/`high` palette and a tileable deterministic `1024×1024` nearest-pixel detail texture adds 4× denser micro-grain without increasing terrain geometry. `flatShading` + gentle height quantization give the cartoon voxel look. A large distant plane fills the fogged horizon. Subscribes to streaming revisions; retains existing chunk geometry from `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/world/geometry.ts`, and only mounts newly prepared terrain meshes. Geometry lifetime belongs to the streaming cache; the detail texture/material are disposed on unmount.
  - `lib/Props.tsx`: Shared instanced geometry for broadleaf/columnar trees and tiered firs with roots, branching trunks and crown highlights; irregular faceted rocks with satellite stones and moss/mineral accents. Fixed-capacity GPU buffers survive streaming shifts; small grass/flower details receive but do not cast shadows. Biome tints color stones and grass. Instance buffers and shared component geometry are disposed on unmount; cleanup calls the prototype disposer because R3F sets the instance `dispose` prop to null.
  - `lib/Pedestals.tsx`: Stone arcade portal frames with emissive inlays, floating mini-game emblems, compact dark billboard plaques, E badges and pulsing activation rings, reading the mutable hovered id each frame. One permanently mounted point light follows the active pedestal; the light count stays constant when chunks enter/leave, avoiding scene-wide shader recompilation stalls.
  - `lib/WorldStreamer.tsx`: Per-frame bridge from the mutable player position to `updateWorldStreaming(x, z)`. When the player enters a new chunk, the deterministic cache generates missing chunks and publishes a new active 8×6 window; old chunks remain cached but are no longer rendered or collidable.
  - `lib/Crates.tsx`: Streamed interactive supply piles rendered in one fixed-capacity instanced mesh. Mutable block state survives nearby chunk shifts via a weak cache keyed by spawn props; evicted regions respawn deterministically. Sleeping piles beyond 18 units skip physics; moving crates continue settling. Player contact uses actual mutable player height, including flight; teleports suppress player/crate contacts. `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/cratePhysics.ts` handles approach-only impacts, stack transfer, bounce and three-axis tumbling. Instance buffers use the prototype disposer on unmount (compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`); crate geometry is also disposed. Shared procedural geometry has inset wood, framing, braces and nails.
  - `lib/CarModel.tsx`: Voxel car with steering/spinning wheels, driven by `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/carPhysics.ts`. Publishes physical player height for crate collisions; follows spring suspension height and inertial pitch/roll; smoothly aligns to slopes while supported and retains its ground orientation in flight. Slope rollover is gated by ground contact and disabled on purpose-built ramps; movement uses the combined terrain/ramp surface. Teleports reset velocity, chassis attitude and contact state. Uses shared obstacle collision and interaction helpers.
  - `lib/CameraRig.tsx`: Third-person follow camera orbits behind the shared player facing for the car, including portal interactions. Shortest-angle exponential smoothing preserves orbit distance through U-turns; per-instance refs avoid stale poses after remount. Tracks physical player height in flight, freezes on Escape, retains pedestal highlighting/tooltips and contextual FOV.
  - `lib/AutoloopBridge.tsx`: Test-only bridge mounted inside the Canvas by `PortfolioPage`. When `?autoloop=1` is present it exposes `window.__autoloop` (`freeze`, `hideHud`, `setSeed`, `setPlayer`, `stats`) and measures FPS. It also sets `gl.info.autoReset = false` and resets the renderer info each frame, so `stats()` reports the whole-frame draw calls/triangles (postprocessing included) instead of only the last pass. Runtime commands validate numeric input and clean up freeze/HUD state on unmount. `freeze(true)` sets a shared flag (`src/lib/autoloop.ts`) that makes `CarModel`, `Crates`, and `CameraRig` skip their `useFrame` updates, producing a static frame for screenshot capture. `hideHud` toggles the `autoloop-hide-hud` class on `<html>`, which hides elements marked with `data-hud` (see `HubHud`).
  - `lib/state.ts`: Mutable game state ref (car position/facing, interaction timer, `interactionTarget`, `hoveredPedestalId`, tooltip). Player includes physical `y` for crate contacts, written by `CarModel.tsx`. Initial `facing` is `Math.PI` so the character starts looking **away** from the follow camera.
  - `lib/controls.ts`: Keyboard input (WASD/arrows for movement/steering, E for interaction).
  - `ui/InteractionTooltip.tsx`: DOM overlay above the hovered pedestal — ref-driven via rAF (no React re-renders).
  - `ui/HubHud.tsx`: Expedition journal, energy and restoration counters, compass and contextual action hints; M toggles a live schematic atlas with selectable targets, crystals and player marker. Uses Effector for discrete progress and mutable refs for continuous position. Carries `data-hud` for the autoloop harness.
  - `ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`: Canvas (fog/sky handled in the scene) + `EffectComposer` (soft `Bloom` + `Vignette`) + HubHud + tooltip + fade overlay + navigation callback (absolute `https://` targets leave the app via `window.location.assign`, relative ones use the router). The fade overlay **slowly brightens to white** during activation (starts at ~25% of the animation, fully white by ~95%) then the route navigates.


- **Ramps** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Ramps.tsx`): Three reusable instanced ramp meshes with curved decks, yellow/cream edge markings, direction arrows and wooden supports. Deck and edge-marking heights follow the analytic ramp profile; buffers survive chunk shifts and use the prototype disposer on unmount, compatible with R3F `dispose={null}`.

- **Expedition HUD** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`): Replaces portfolio branding with expedition journal, energy, progress, notices, context action hints and compass distance. M opens a schematic atlas with remaining crystals, beacon status and selectable destinations. Player marker and proximity text are ref-driven; responsive keyboard-accessible panels.


- **HUD and Escape menu** (`src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`): Atlas stays live and has no close icon; circular survey map and compact destination rows. Escape opens a focus-trapped pause dialog with controls (portal hint removed), resume button and persistent transparency slider. Continuous navigation/prompt text remains ref-driven; only Escape controls mutable pause. Bottom overlay contains contextual interaction only.

- **Paused world ornaments** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/Pedestals.tsx`): Portal emblem and light animation clocks advance only during gameplay, so Escape freezes their pose and resumes without a time jump.

- **Car-only scene** (`src/pages/Portfolio/lib/TownScene.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/PortfolioPage.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/ui/HubHud.tsx`, `src/pages/Portfolio/lib/state.ts`): CarModel is the sole player entity; no character selector, toggle event, selection prop/type, walking animation state or switching hint remains.
